# ADR-001: Product Engineering Constitution

Status: Accepted

## Context

Project knowledge and engineering decisions should remain reusable and available to both humans and AI agents.

One-off instructions provided only through prompts are difficult to reuse, review and version.

## Decision

Kopernik will use a version-controlled Product Engineering Constitution as a primary source of engineering and product rules.

`AGENTS.md` defines how the agent works.

The Constitution defines the principles the project follows.

ADRs record significant decisions and their rationale.

## Consequences

Project rules become reviewable and versioned.

Agent behaviour can be evaluated against explicit rules.

Maintaining the Constitution becomes part of product development.
